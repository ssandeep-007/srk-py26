Remove chars from odd indices
----------------------------------------------------------

WAP to read a string from user, and implement the logic to 
remove the characters which are in the odd numbers of the 
index. 
Testcase 1: 
Enter String 
abcdefgh 
Original String:  abcdefgh 
After Elimination: aceg
